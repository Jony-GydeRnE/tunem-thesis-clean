Scope: design, commercialization and internationalization of Chinese spherical tokamaks, connecting SUNIST-2, Startorus and SNERDI.

The 19-page proposal and one-page summary are preserved documents, except that the student ID is removed from the public proposal PDF and source. The originals remain outside this repository. Their working sources are included; byte-for-byte equivalence after rebuilding is not assumed. All four referenced images are in `assets/`.

Build the proposal with a Unicode-capable TeX engine and **Noto Serif CJK SC**. Preserve Chinese text, citations and image attribution. Third-party photos and logos retain their original rights; this repository grants no additional reuse permission. Their redistribution permissions have not been independently verified. Keep submitted PDFs unchanged; new builds go to `.build/` for review.
