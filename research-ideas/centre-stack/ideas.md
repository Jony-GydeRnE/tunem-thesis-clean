# Centre-stack idea catalogue

One-line research questions; preserved identifiers do not establish novelty or feasibility.

## Startup and compatibility

- **CS01 — Startup–NI-HTS compatibility:** calculate which winding currents, losses, and field errors a representative merging/PF transient causes.
- **CS02 — Solenoid-equivalent merging benefit:** measure delivered plasma state, saved CS volt-seconds, energy cost, and shot reliability.
- **CS03 / H1 — Startup allocation:** test whether CS-heavy, hybrid, or merge-heavy operation best satisfies one common mission.
- **CS04 — Loss-channel triage:** distinguish hysteresis, structural eddy, contact, coupling, and joint penalties before choosing a design lever.
- **CS05 — Contact grading:** optimize a few manufacturable resistance zones only when contact-sensitive response is consequential.
- **CS06 — Tape orientation:** reduce the relevant hysteretic response through conductor orientation and field-angle control.
- **CS07 — Passive-structure segmentation:** interrupt harmful eddy-current loops while preserving required plasma-field penetration.
- **CS08 — Actuator placement:** change spatial coupling between startup fields, plasma, casing, and the cold winding.
- **CS09 — Waveform co-design:** shape startup drive subject to plasma targets, voltage/current limits, and actual induced response.
- **CS10 — Recovery and cadence:** find repetition limits from cold energy, thermal/electrical recovery, cyclic loads, and dwell.
- **CS11 — Internal-actuator survival:** test whether moving startup hardware inboard merely transfers the obstacle into radiation and maintenance.
- **CS12 — Reduced compatibility atlas:** map feasible startup allocation versus repetition rate before adding aspect ratio.
- **CS13 — Physical marginal values:** quantify the value of a millimetre, weber, cold joule, or outage day without unsupported dollar precision.
- **CS14 — Formation/burn-through branch:** pivot toward plasma-formation variability when it dominates the magnet penalty and usable data exist.

## Architecture options

- **AR01 — Stratified design family:** compare discrete architectures whose topology, variables, and constraints can differ.
- **AR02 — Burden transfer:** require every improvement to identify both the burden removed and the place it reappears.
- **AR03 — Functional centre stack:** classify required functions as pinned, movable, shared, exportable, deletable, or replaceable.
- **AR04 — Two linkage requirements:** distinguish compulsory TF current linkage from startup flux that may be supplied by different carriers.
- **AR05 — Spatial/energy/lifetime ledgers:** require fit, tolerable power consumption, and usable lifetime separately.
- **AR06 / H0 — Constraint migration:** test whether higher cadence moves the active limit from radial/startup constraints to recovery/fatigue.
- **AR07 — Winding family (N, σt):** vary turn topology and transverse conductance without treating a monolith as the same circuit at infinite conductance.
- **AR08 — Anisotropic winding inverse design:** design a spatial conductance field against the actual startup response.
- **AR09 — Local spectral reduction:** use relaxation modes and forcing overlaps as diagnostics, without assuming global linearity or independent knobs.
- **AR10 / H2 — Selective electromagnetic transmission:** retain useful plasma-field transfer while reducing harmful winding response and preserving protection.
- **AR11 — Bandwidth allocation:** assign fields and protective responses to components by timescale without screening the desired startup field.
- **AR12 / H3 — Selectivity obstruction:** seek a passive/reciprocal response bound after a robust physical tradeoff appears.
- **AR13 — Replaceable cartridge:** trade shielding, component lifetime, replacement work, and outage cost within one mission.
- **AR14 — Composite TF circuit:** compare a resistive inner leg with HTS outer returns and explicitly priced joints.
- **AR15 — Shared structure and shielding:** make one justified region carry mechanical load and attenuate neutrons.
- **AR16 — Chilled resistive centrepost:** optimize conductor temperature with resistivity, field, cooling, pumping, and duty costs.
- **AR17 — Liquid-metal multifunctional post:** explore integrated conductor/coolant/breeding functions while accounting for MHD drag and materials limits.
- **AR18 — Plasma centrepost:** replace the material linking-current carrier only through a separately validated confinement architecture.
- **AR19 / R4 — Controlled stochastic exhaust:** shape an edge transport layer toward useful outboard exhaust while testing the inboard-lobe constraint.
- **AR20 — Campaign-held TF:** amortize a long magnet charge over repeated events while keeping the winding energized.
- **AR21 — Triangularity-sign branch:** compare positive/negative triangularity as architecture choices under the same requirements.
- **AR22 — Sharp-D corner control:** test whether local geometry/contact changes reduce a consequential current or stress concentration.
- **AR23 — Factory-versus-bespoke economics:** include manufacturing learning and maintainability only after physical architecture costs are credible.
- **AR24 — Integrated cartridge functions:** test whether conductor, case, vacuum boundary, and service interfaces can share a maintainable centre column.

## Bounds and alternative carriers

- **DS01 — Flux-aware area bound:** include minimum CS flux area alongside TF and support areas in a necessary aspect-ratio screen.
- **DS02 — Nuclear-crowding coordinate:** expose how nuclear/interface allocations consume the available inboard width.
- **DS03 — Robust closure margins:** compare space, startup/exergy, and lifetime margins under uncertain inputs.
- **DS04 — Alternative startup carriers:** compare CS, RF/beam ramp, coaxial helicity injection, and merging against the actual startup requirement.
- **DS05 — Nuclear lifetime and breeding allocation:** evaluate shielding and exportable breeding against tritium, fluence, replacement, and availability.
