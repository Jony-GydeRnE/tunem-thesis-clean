## Three concrete discussions for Professor Tan

1. **Startup–HTS compatibility:** quantify loss and field error from one representative startup waveform.
2. **Solenoid-equivalent benefit:** measure the CS flux genuinely saved at the same achieved plasma state.
3. **Hybrid startup allocation:** compare CS-heavy, hybrid and merge-heavy designs after counting recovered space, heat and recovery time.

These are proposed projects, not claims of novelty. Begin with available data and a reproducible baseline. [Three-page prospectus](centre-stack/research-prospectus.pdf) ([source](centre-stack/research-prospectus.tex)).

## Higher-risk directions

- **Selective field transmission:** preserve useful startup coupling while reducing harmful winding response.
- **Physically constrained flux networks:** test whether independent conserved structures can stabilize a network before considering fusion.
- **Frontier:** a synthetic confining medium whose preferred defects form plasma-carrying Y-junctions. Fascinating, but no validated medium or reactor exists in this programme.

The catalogue preserves the broader ideas as hypotheses. Detailed conversation-based files are not included; clean notes retain substantive questions and declared model limits. New results need assumptions, evidence and a falsifiable test—not stronger adjectives.
