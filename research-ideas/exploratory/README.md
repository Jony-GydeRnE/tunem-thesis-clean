# Exploratory directions

[Fusion and mathematical ideas](ideas.md) · [Foundational extensions](foundations.md)

These are questions, not demonstrated technologies. The catalogue includes nuclear-state preparation, transport control, synthetic confinement, junction networks and categorical descriptions. QCD/gravity extensions are retained separately; they are not prerequisites for the thesis or the first centre-stack project.

For any branch, specify a physical model, accessible observable, decisive failure test and cost of preparation/control. A useful analogy is not a mechanism; a mathematical invariant is not automatically a confinement benefit. No novelty or feasibility claim follows from inclusion here.
