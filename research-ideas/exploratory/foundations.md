# Foundational extensions

One-line research questions; preserved identifiers do not establish novelty or feasibility.

## Foundational and transport questions

- **CI01 — Coupled topological ledgers:** investigate a common bookkeeping structure for paired invariants across gauge theory, plasma, and double copy.
- **CI02 / M0.1 — Gauge-stack measure:** formulate lattice gauge fields and reflection positivity through refinement/descent without discarding singular strata.
- **CI03 / M0.2 — Uniform continuum control:** seek multiscale estimates through the weak-coupling/continuum crossover.
- **CI04 / M0.3 — Reconstruction:** recover a Hilbert space and Hamiltonian from a properly established positive measure.
- **CI05 — Stochastic-quantization route:** investigate a gauge-covariant SPDE route to the target invariant measure.
- **CI06 / T1 — Vortex energy–entropy bound:** test a coupling-uniform disorder/percolation mechanism for confinement.
- **CI07 / D1 — G2 discriminator:** require the mechanism to distinguish screening/intermediate confinement from center-based asymptotic confinement.
- **CI08 / D2 — k-string crossover:** test the transition between intermediate representation dependence and asymptotic N-ality behavior.
- **CI09 / D3 — Chiral linkage:** investigate what a proposed confinement mechanism predicts for chiral structure.
- **CI10 — Gap from clustering:** seek uniform decay/functional inequalities leading to a reconstructed spectral gap without assuming it from an area law.
- **CI11 — Singular-stratum geometry:** investigate a justified Poincaré/log-Sobolev route rather than treating a curvature analogy as a gap proof.
- **CI12 / R1 — Diophantine q-profile:** test whether rational-surface placement can improve a specified transport-barrier regime.
- **CI13 / R2 — Staircase pinning:** test whether an engineered rational-surface lattice stabilizes E×B shear barriers against turbulent erasure.
- **CI14 / R3 — Helicity/barrier co-design:** seek a consistent reduced variational model joining current relaxation and transport-barrier structure.
- **CI15 / R5 — Residue feedback:** investigate whether a measurable estimate of field-line order can drive useful real-time edge control.
- **CI16 / P1 — Measure-level double copy:** ask whether a nonperturbative pairing reproduces a gravitational measure and identifies its field content.
- **CI17 / P2 — Self-dual test:** compare a tractable double-copy construction with specified known algebra/anomaly targets.
- **CI18 / P3 — Gravity energy–entropy bound:** test a gravitational defect argument against quantitative consistency targets.
- **CI19 / P4 — Inherited positivity:** investigate whether a justified pairing can control the gravitational conformal-mode problem.
- **CI20 — Gauge/gravity defect dictionary:** test, rather than assume, vortex–Misner-string, gap–energy-floor, and confinement–singularity correspondences.
